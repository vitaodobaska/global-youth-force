# Youth Force (Global Youth Foundation) — Reference Dossier & Branding

This document consolidates all intelligence, content, and visual identity extracted from the official website [globalyouthforce.org](https://globalyouthforce.org), collected via **Firecrawl MCP**. It serves as the foundational context and strategic direction for the design and development of the new cinematic landing page.

---

## 1. Organization Overview

* **Brand Name:** Youth Force
* **Legal Name / Foundation:** Global Youth Foundation
* **Core Purpose / Main Tagline:**
  > *"Empowering young leaders to be a force for good in their world for Jesus."*
* **Central Mission:**
  - **Empowering Young Leaders:** Awakening and empowering the next generation for impactful leadership in the Kingdom and in society.
  - **Equipping Youth Leaders:** Equipping pastors, mentors, and youth workers with training and support so they become healthy influences in young leaders' lives.

---

## 2. Methodological Pillars & Philosophy

Youth Force operates around a 4-stage leadership development funnel:

1. **Awaken:** Awaken young leaders to an authentic relationship with Jesus.
2. **Activate:** Activate them to dream and pursue what God is calling them to.
3. **Engage, Equip & Encourage:** Provide continuous mentorship, leadership tools, and practical encouragement on the journey.
4. **Connect:** Connect them to a community of growth and global reach.

### Key Quotes & Core Beliefs
* *"A leader is great not because of their power but because of their ability to empower others."* — John Maxwell.
* *"We believe we are better together!"* — Principle of strategic inter-denominational ministerial collaboration.
* **Humility & Disinterested Leadership:** Healthy leadership operates through "disinterested love" (Philippians 2), where the leader serves others without self-promotion.
* **Faithfulness in Ministry:** Focus on the daily calling and consistency, trusting the final results to God.
* **Disciple-Making Culture (Great Commission):** Every individual Christian is called to be a disciple-maker.

## 4. Core Programs

### A. Internship Program
* **Duration:** 8 to 10 weeks (mid-May through mid-August).
* **Audience:** Primarily university students.
* **Locations:** Cabo San Lucas, Mexico and Chicago, IL.
* **Specialty Tracks (10 weeks):**
  - Social Media
  - Music & Arts
  - Operations
  - Writing & Content Creation
  - Videography
  - Marketing & Business
* **Financial Model:** $500 participation fee, with fundraising support available through Youth Force.

### B. Residency Program
* **Duration:** 1 to 2 years with a formal curriculum of learning, mentorship, and hands-on ministry experience.
* **Locations:** Myrtle Beach, SC or Chicago, IL.
* **Practical Training Components:**
  1. *Specialized Training:* Instruction in a specialty area determined prior to the program start.
  2. *Leadership Development:* Ongoing process alongside youth workers from around the world.
  3. *Personal Mentoring & Coaching:* Strengths-based coaching and spiritual formation mentorship.
  4. *Preaching:* Coaching and opportunities to preach in Youth Force venues.
  5. *Work Study:* Average of 28 hours/week with an annual stipend and personalized leadership study plan.
  6. *Time Off:* 3 weeks of vacation per year (with YF Leadership Team approval).

### C. Events, Conferences & Missions
* Short-term mission trips coordinated for teams and students.
* Immersive leadership activation experiences.

### D. Resources for Youth Leaders & Families
* **The 21 Day Leader Challenge:** Created by Tony Rogatto to equip Next Gen ministry volunteers to better engage, care for, and encourage kids, students, and families. Includes guides, training videos, editable documents, and printable materials.
* **Discipleship & Bible Reading Plans:** Historical presence of practical plans (David, Isaiah, Esther reading plans), available in multiple languages including Portuguese.

The organization operates in consortium with major global evangelical ministries:

1. **Global Leadership Summit - Next Gen (GLS NextGen USA):**
   - A transformational leadership experience designed to awaken students' leadership, faith, and life journey.

   - Global ministry representing local churches and evangelical alliances in 140+ countries, seeking justice and transformation.

   - Strategic cooperation and unity network across the Asian continent and beyond, expanding the Kingdom of God.
   - Website: [asiaevangelicals.org](https://asiaevangelicals.org/)
5. **ICETE (International Council for Evangelical Theological Education):**
   - Global community advancing quality and collaboration in evangelical theological education worldwide.

## 7. Design Opportunities for the New Platform

1. **Full Visual Modernization:** The current site runs on an outdated Joomla/Bootstrap 3 structure with a rigid layout and a flat blue palette with no cinematic depth.
2. **Stronger Narrative Architecture:** Integrate the 3 major fronts — *Internship/Residency*, *Global Partnerships*, and *NextGen Leader Resources* — into interactive component patterns (shuffler cards, telemetry feed, scheduler) as outlined in the cinematic design guidelines.
3. **Interactivity & Engagement:** Bring modern application forms for internships and residency, plus direct leadership connection flows.
4. **Multi-language Presence:** The existing site already offers content in English, Spanish, German, Italian, and French — a multilingual structure should be considered in the new architecture.
